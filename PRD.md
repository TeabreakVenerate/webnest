# Webnest - Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Objective
Webnest is a high-conversion, productized storefront agency providing fast e-commerce setups for campus entrepreneurs and local retail boutiques within 48 hours. 

## 2. Target Audience
Student entrepreneurs and local retail owners who require a structured digital presence without monthly SaaS fees.

## 3. Core Features & User Flow
*   **1. The Hook (Single-Column Hero):** A high-impact, centered landing section promising a fully operational store for ₦10,000 in 48 hours.
*   **2. Proof Inspection (Showcase):** A visual grid displaying live case studies using minimalist URL bar frames containing storefront screenshots.
*   **3. Unified Pricing & Add-ons Grid:** An interactive, merged pricing engine allowing users to select the Base Package and toggle add-ons seamlessly without cluttered simulated software widgets.
*   **4. Structured Intake Form:** Captures vendor details (Name, Brand, Niche, WhatsApp/Phone, Email, Requirements).
*   **5. Dual Dispatch Checkout:**
    *   *Email Payload:* Background AJAX push to Formsubmit.
    *   *WhatsApp Routing:* Auto-redirection to a parameterized `wa.me` link with a summarized order brief.
*   **6. Client-Side Ledger:** Order history saved to `localStorage`.
